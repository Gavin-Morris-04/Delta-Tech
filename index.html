Questions

Things owners usually ask first.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly

Do I need to be technical to work with you?

Not at all. You know your business; we handle the technology. We'll ask about your day-to-day work, not your tech stack, and every proposal is written in plain English.

What does a custom app or website cost?

It depends on what you need, so we don't publish a price list. After a short call we send a written proposal with the scope, price and timeline before any work begins.

If an off-the-shelf tool would serve you better than a custom build, we'll tell you.

Why custom software instead of something off the shelf?

Off-the-shelf tools are great when they fit. Our HVAC client found the options were either too expensive or didn't match how the team worked, and didn't want a monthly subscription for features nobody would use. A focused custom build fixed that.

Will AI replace my office staff?

That's not how we build it. Our automation handles sorting and suggestions so your office spends less time on busywork, and a person approves every decision that matters, like creating a job or contacting a customer.

What happens after launch?

We stay on. We help your team get comfortable with the new system, fix issues, and make improvements as your business changes.

Do you only work with businesses in Louisiana?

Louisiana is home and where most of our clients are, but we can work with businesses anywhere over video calls and email.
